## 📱 Setup on Your Phone (iPhone & Android)

You can use ShopWrench right in your phone's browser, or add it to your home screen so it opens full-screen just like a native app—**no App Store or Google Play download required!**

### 🍏 On iPhone (Apple iOS / Safari)
1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone and go to your ShopWrench link.
2. At the bottom of the screen, tap the **Share** button (the square icon with an arrow pointing up).
3. In the menu that pops up, scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. (Optional) Check the name (it will say **ShopWrench**) and tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. You will now see the **ShopWrench** icon on your home screen. Tap it anytime to open it full-screen without web browser address bars.

### 🤖 On Android (Google Chrome)
1. Open **Chrome** on your Android phone and go to your ShopWrench link.
2. Tap the **"Install App"** button that appears in the app header (or tap the **three vertical dots** in the top-right corner of Chrome).
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **"Install"**.
5. The **ShopWrench** icon is now added to your home screen and app drawer.

### 📷 Camera & Microphone Permissions (Important)
- **Camera**: When you tap **"Scan Barcode"** to read a VIN door sticker for the first time, your phone will ask: *"Allow ShopWrench to access your camera?"* Tap **Allow**.
- **Microphone**: When you tap the **microphone icon** to speak notes or read a VIN aloud with Voice VIN, your phone will ask for microphone access. Tap **Allow**.

## 💻 Setup on Your PC / Laptop / Mac

ShopWrench works on any computer with an internet browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox, or Brave).

1. Open your browser and navigate to your ShopWrench URL.
2. Bookmark the page (`Ctrl + D` on Windows / `Cmd + D` on Mac) so you can get back to it quickly.

   - Click **Install**. ShopWrench will run in its own clean window on your desktop, and you can pin it to your Windows Taskbar or Mac Dock.

## ☁️ How to Log In & Sync Phone and PC

ShopWrench automatically saves your jobs locally so you never lose data. When you log in, all your vehicles, parts lists, and notes **sync across your phone and PC in real time**. A job you start at the car on your phone is immediately visible on your shop office computer.

### Step 1: Open the Sync Menu
In the top-right corner of the screen, click or tap the button with the cloud icon:
- If you're not logged in, it will say **"Sync Phone & PC"** in yellow/amber.
- If you are already logged in, it will say **"Cloud Synced"** with a green pulsing dot.

### Step 2: Choose How to Sign In
You have two easy choices:
1. **Google Sign-In (Recommended)**:
   - Click or tap **"Sign In with Google"**.
   - Select your Google / Gmail account from the popup.
   - You're instantly logged in!
2. **Email & Password**:
   - Click the **Email** option.
   - Enter your email address and choose a password (minimum 6 characters).
   - If this is your first time, click **"Need an account? Sign Up"**. If you already made one, click **"Log In"**.

### Step 3: Connect Your Second Device
1. Open ShopWrench on your other device (for example, your office laptop).
2. Tap the **"Sync Phone & PC"** button in the top header.
3. Sign into the **exact same account** you used on your phone.
4. **Done!** Any job, part status update, or note made on one device will show up on the other device within seconds.
5. **Works Offline Too**: If Wi-Fi drops out while you're under the hood, your work is saved locally on your device and will automatically sync to the cloud as soon as connection is restored.

## 🚗 Example Walkthrough: Starting a New Job

Here is a step-by-step example of how to check in a vehicle and manage a repair job from start to finish.

### Scenario
> A customer brings in a **2018 GMC Canyon** with the check engine light on, running rough, and running hotter than normal.

---

### Step 1: Open New Intake
- On your phone or PC, click or tap **"New Intake"** (or the **"+"** icon in the navigation bar).

---

### Step 2: Enter the VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)
Choose whichever method is fastest for you:
- **Option A (Barcode Scanner - Easiest on phone)**:
  - Tap **"Scan Barcode"**.
  - Point your phone camera at the 17-character barcode on the driver's door jamb sticker.
  - The scanner beeps, captures the VIN, and automatically starts decoding it.
- **Option B (Voice VIN - When your hands are greasy)**:
  - Tap **"Voice VIN"** and speak the 17 letters and numbers clearly (e.g., *"One G T E..."*).
- **Option C (Type Manually)**:
  - Type the 17-character VIN in the box (e.g. `1GTN6DE19J1168641`).
  - *(Tip: For quick testing, you can tap one of the 1-click sample vehicle buttons like "2018 GMC Canyon")*.

---

### Step 3: Decode Vehicle Specs
- Tap the blue **"Decode VIN"** button.
- In less than one second, ShopWrench contacts the official **U.S. Department of Transportation (NHTSA)** database.
- The screen displays the decoded vehicle information:
  - **Year**: 2018
  - **Make**: GMC
  - **Model**: CANYON
  - **Engine**: 3.6L V6 (Gasoline)
  - **Transmission & Drive Type**: Automatic, 4WD / RWD

---

### Step 4: Add Customer & Service Notes
- **Customer Name**: Type the customer's name (e.g., *Sarah Johnson*).
- **Service Notes / Complaint**: Enter the symptoms. You can type or tap the **microphone icon** to dictate:
  > *"Customer reports check engine light illuminated on highway, temperature gauge fluctuating high, heater blowing lukewarm."*
- **Initial Parts Needed** *(Optional)*: Enter any parts you already know you'll need, separated by commas:
  > `Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor, Thermostat, 50/50 Dex-Cool Antifreeze`

---

### Step 5: Launch the Repair Job
- Tap the amber **"Launch Repair Job"** button at the bottom of the form.
- The new job is created and you are brought directly into the **Job Workspace**.

---

### Step 6: Log OBD-II Trouble Codes
1. Under the **OBD-II Diagnostics** section of the job, click **"Add OBD Code"**.
2. Type in the code retrieved from your scan tool (e.g. `P0128`).
3. Click **"Add"**.
4. The system displays:
   - **Code Name**: *Coolant Thermostat (Coolant Temperature Below Thermostat Regulating Temperature)*
   - **Symptoms**: Engine running cold, poor fuel economy, check engine light on.
   - **Common Causes**: Stuck-open thermostat, faulty coolant temperature sensor, low coolant level.

---

### Step 7: Source Parts with 1-Click Store Links
Under the **Parts Needed** list, you will see each part you added. Beside each part are direct sourcing buttons:

- **AutoZone**: Opens AutoZone with the **2018 GMC Canyon 3.6L** and part name pre-searched.
- **O'Reilly**: Opens O'Reilly Auto Parts pre-filtered to the vehicle and part.
- **Advance Auto**: Opens Advance Auto Parts pre-filtered to the vehicle and part.
- **RockAuto**: Jumps directly to the vehicle tree for **GMC ➔ 2018 ➔ Canyon** on `rockauto.com`, and automatically copies the exact part name to your clipboard so you can paste it right into RockAuto's search or filter box!
- **Prices**: Opens Google Shopping to compare prices across retail suppliers instantly.
- **Suppliers**: Opens a menu for custom local parts distributors, NAPA, eBay Motors, or salvage yards (**Car-Part.com**, **LKQ**) for used or hard-to-find components.

---

### Step 8: Track Part Orders & Workflow
As parts move through your shop, tap the status pill for each part:
- 🟡 **Needed** ➔ Part needs to be ordered.
- 🔵 **Ordered** ➔ Part has been purchased or ordered from supplier.
- 🟣 **Arrived** ➔ Delivery driver dropped it off at the shop bay.
- 🟢 **Installed** ➔ Part is bolted onto the vehicle and tested.

---

### Step 9: Print Estimate or Work Order
- At the top-right of the job workspace, click the **printer icon** (**"Print Estimate"**).
- A clean, professional invoice/estimate opens formatted for standard 8.5" x 11" paper or PDF export, containing:
  - Vehicle specifications and VIN
  - Customer complaint and diagnostic notes
  - Itemized parts list with prices and quantities
  - Labor hours, hourly shop rate, and total cost calculation
- Hand it to the customer or save it as a PDF for your records!

## 💡 Troubleshooting & Helpful Tips

### Barcode Scanning
- **Lighting**: If the door jamb is dark or under a car lift, turn on your phone's flashlight or hold a work light near the barcode.
- **Distance**: Hold your phone camera about 4 to 8 inches away from the barcode so the red guideline is centered over the lines.
- **Worn Stickers**: If a door jamb sticker is torn or faded, use **Voice VIN** or type the VIN from the lower corner of the front windshield.

### Cloud Sync & Login
- **Same Account**: Ensure both your phone and PC are logged into the **same Google account or email address**.
- **Check Status**: Look at the cloud button in the top-right. A green icon with **"Cloud Synced"** means live sync is active.
- **Offline Mode**: If you see a yellow cloud icon, your changes are safely stored on your device and will push to the cloud as soon as you have internet access.

### Custom Suppliers
- In any job, click **"Suppliers"** ➔ **"Add Custom Store"** to save your favorite local parts vendors, machine shops, or dealership wholesale counters.
